# CarPapers - Google Play store listing

Texts for Play Console → Grow users → Store listing. English for the US app
(`com.carpapers.app`), Spanish for Mexico/Argentina (`com.carpapers.mx`,
`com.carpapers.ar`). The app never mentions traffic fines, so neither does
the listing.

## English (en-US)

**App name** (max 30): `CarPapers: Car Expense Tracker`

**Short description** (max 80):

Track car expenses, fuel, service and insurance, with reminders you won't miss.

**Full description** (max 4000):

```
CarPapers keeps everything about your car in one place: what you spend, when service is due, and when your insurance runs out.

TRACK EVERY EXPENSE
• Fuel, service, repairs, tuning, car wash and more
• Spending by month and by category, with clear charts
• Fuel economy in MPG or L/100 km, and your cost per mile

NEVER MISS A DATE
• Reminders for insurance renewal and scheduled maintenance
• Service schedule by mileage and by date

ALL YOUR CARS
• Several vehicles in one garage, each with its own photo and history
• Your data is backed up and synced to your account

HANDY EXTRAS
• Find gas stations, car washes and service stations near you
• Export your history to PDF or CSV, or prepare a report for a buyer when you sell your car
• Miles or kilometers, gallons or liters, and your currency

Free to download. An optional subscription is available in the app.

Questions or ideas? Write to carpapers.support@gmail.com
```
